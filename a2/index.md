# A2

A2 starts where A1 ends, with the basic sentence and everyday words in place. It widens the language to the past, comparisons and the reasons behind what people do.

## What learners know

- The accusative and dative in depth, with the prepositions that take them.
- Adjective endings, and comparisons with *als* and *wie*.
- Subordinate clauses with *weil*, *dass*, *wenn* and *als*, and relative clauses.
- Polite wishes and advice with the Konjunktiv II.
- Reflexive verbs, and verbs that take a fixed preposition.

## What learners can do

- Describe daily life, family, work and surroundings in simple terms.
- Compare people, places and things.
- Give simple reasons for a choice or a plan.
- Make polite requests and give simple advice.
- Ask indirect questions, such as *Können Sie mir sagen, wo der Bahnhof ist?*
- Understand short, clear texts and conversations on familiar topics.
