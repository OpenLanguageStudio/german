# Everyday verbs 1

Ten verbs carry an astonishing share of everyday German: doing, going, coming, drinking, living, working. All ten in this pack behave regularly in the present tense, so your ear can settle into one clean pattern while the words themselves become automatic. Each verb comes in two different persons, because a verb you only know as *ich mache* is only half a verb.

## The words

| German | English |
|---|---|
| machen | to do, to make |
| gehen | to go, to walk |
| kommen | to come |
| trinken | to drink |
| wohnen | to live (somewhere) |
| arbeiten | to work |
| kaufen | to buy |
| lernen | to learn |
| suchen | to look for |
| finden | to find |

One small wrinkle worth hearing: *arbeiten* inserts an extra e in the *du* form (*du arbeitest*), the regular pattern for stems ending in -t.

## Hear it in practice

Each verb comes as a pair of sentences in two different persons: *ich* one moment, *du* or *ihr* the next. Across the ten pairs you hear every conjugation slot German has, and a short review coda at the end replays four of the verbs in forms you know from the grammar albums.

Was machst du heute?
: What are you doing today?

Er macht das Frühstück.
: He makes breakfast.

Wir gehen heute ins Kino.
: We are going to the cinema today.

Geht ihr auch ins Schwimmbad?
: Are you going to the pool too?

Kommst du aus Berlin?
: Are you from Berlin?

Kommen Sie bitte mit.
: Please come with me.

Ich trinke morgens Kaffee.
: I drink coffee in the morning.

Er trinkt gern Tee.
: He likes drinking tea.

Wir wohnen in Hamburg.
: We live in Hamburg.

Wo wohnen Sie?
: Where do you live?

Ich arbeite von neun bis fünf.
: I work from nine to five.

Du arbeitest zu viel.
: You work too much.

Ich kaufe Äpfel.
: I am buying apples.

Er kauft einen Kuchen.
: He is buying a cake.

Wir lernen zusammen Deutsch.
: We are learning German together.

Du lernst schnell.
: You learn quickly.

Ich suche meinen Schlüssel.
: I am looking for my key.

Sie sucht eine Wohnung.
: She is looking for a flat.

Ich finde den Bahnhof nicht.
: I can't find the station.

Er findet seine Brille nicht.
: He can't find his glasses.

Was hast du gestern gemacht?
: What did you do yesterday?

Ich habe Brot gekauft.
: I bought bread.

Komm bitte her!
: Come here, please!

Wir haben Deutsch gelernt.
: We learned German.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- Moving: *gehen, kommen*.
- Daily life: *trinken, wohnen*.
- Doing and getting: *machen, arbeiten, lernen, kaufen*.
- The seeker pair: *suchen* (looking) and *finden* (finding).
- The vowel-changing verbs have their own pack: [Everyday verbs 2](/a1/vocabulary/everyday-verbs-2.md).
:::
