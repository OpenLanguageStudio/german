# Conjunctions und, oder, aber, denn

Four small words join your sentences into longer thoughts: *und* (and), *oder* (or), *aber* (but), *denn* (because). Their best feature: they change nothing. Both halves keep normal word order, verb in second position on each side.

## Position zero

Think of these conjunctions as sitting in position zero: outside the sentence, before slot 1. That is why the verb stays exactly where it was.

| Sentence 1 | Position 0 | Sentence 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Ich trinke Kaffee, | und | er trinkt Tee. |
| Ich möchte kommen, | aber | ich habe keine Zeit. |
| Ich bleibe zu Hause, | denn | ich bin krank. |

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich trinke Kaffee {conj}`und` er trinkt Tee.
: I drink coffee and he drinks tea.

Ich möchte kommen, {conj}`aber` ich habe keine Zeit.
: I would like to come, but I have no time.
:::

## Joining words and phrases

*Und* and *oder* also join single words, with no comma: *Brot und Käse*, *Tee oder Kaffee*.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Wir kaufen Brot {conj}`und` Käse.
: We buy bread and cheese.

Nimmst du den Bus {conj}`oder` das Fahrrad?
: Are you taking the bus or the bike?

Das Hotel ist klein, {conj}`aber` schön.
: The hotel is small but nice.
:::

## Reasons with *denn*

*Denn* introduces a reason and is the easiest "because" in German, precisely because word order stays untouched. (Its cousin *weil* rearranges the sentence; that is A2 territory.)

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich bleibe zu Hause, {conj}`denn` ich bin krank.
: I am staying home because I am ill.

Ich gehe ins Bett, {conj}`denn` es ist spät.
: I am going to bed because it is late.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- *und, oder, aber, denn* join sentences without touching word order.
- They sit in position zero; the verb stays second on both sides.
- *und* and *oder* also join single words, comma-free.
- *denn* = because, the A1-friendly way to give reasons.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Ich bleibe zu Hause, denn bin ich krank.* → denn **ich bin** krank. (no inversion after denn)
- *\*Er ist nett aber, sehr müde.* → Er ist nett, **aber** sehr müde. (comma before aber)
- Reaching for *weil* and rearranging: at A1, *denn* does the job with normal word order.
:::
