# Modal Verbs dürfen and sollen

:::{admonition} Prerequisites
:class: note
Before reading this article, make sure you are comfortable with:
- [A1 > Grammar > Modal Verbs können, müssen, wollen](/a1/grammar/verbs/koennen-muessen-wollen.md)
:::

Two more modals complete the A1 set. *Dürfen* handles permission: what is allowed and what is forbidden. *Sollen* carries someone else's wish, the sense of being supposed to do something. Both use the familiar bracket, with the main verb at the end.

## The forms

| Person | dürfen | sollen |
|---|---|---|
| ich | darf | soll |
| du | darfst | sollst |
| er / sie / es | darf | soll |
| wir | dürfen | sollen |
| ihr | dürft | sollt |
| sie / Sie | dürfen | sollen |

*Sollen* is the easy one: no vowel change anywhere.

## *dürfen*: permission and prohibition

*Darf ich ...?* is the standard way to ask permission. The negative *darf nicht* means something is forbidden, which is stronger than English "may not" suggests.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
{verb}`Darf` ich hier **parken**?
: May I park here?

Du {verb}`darfst` hier nicht **rauchen**.
: You are not allowed to smoke here.

Die Kinder {verb}`dürfen` heute Abend **fernsehen**.
: The children are allowed to watch TV tonight.
:::

## *sollen*: what someone else wants

*Sollen* reports an instruction or expectation from outside: the doctor, the boss, your grandmother. As a question, *Soll ich ...?* offers help.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Der Arzt sagt, ich {verb}`soll` viel **schlafen**.
: The doctor says I should sleep a lot.

{verb}`Soll` ich dir **helfen**?
: Should I help you?

Wir {verb}`sollen` um acht Uhr da **sein**.
: We are supposed to be there at eight.
:::

:::{tip}
*Muss nicht* and *darf nicht* are worlds apart: *Du musst nicht kommen* means you don't have to come; *Du darfst nicht kommen* means you are not allowed to. Mixing them up changes an option into a ban.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- *Darf ich ...?* asks permission; *darf nicht* means forbidden.
- *Sollen* passes on someone else's wish; *Soll ich ...?* offers help.
- Forms: *ich darf / ich soll*; ich and er forms match, as with all modals.
- *muss nicht* = don't have to; *darf nicht* = must not.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*er darfst* → er **darf**. (darfst belongs to du)
- *\*Ich darf nicht kommen* meant as "I don't have to" → Ich **muss nicht** kommen. (darf nicht = forbidden)
- *\*Soll ich helfe dir?* → Soll ich dir **helfen**? (infinitive at the end)
:::
