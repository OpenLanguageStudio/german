# B2

B2 turns correct German into precise German. It starts from a secure B1 and adds the structures of formal speech and writing, from nominal style to indirect speech.

## What learners know

- Nominalisation, and the noun-verb phrases of formal German.
- Indirect speech, and the different ways to form the passive.
- The rarer connectors, such as two-part and infinitive connectors.
- Modal particles, and word order in complex sentences.
- Prepositions with the genitive, and relative clauses beyond the basics.

## What learners can do

- Follow the main ideas of complex text on concrete and abstract topics.
- Take part in conversation with enough ease for regular contact with native speakers.
- Write clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects.
- Explain a viewpoint and weigh its advantages and disadvantages.
