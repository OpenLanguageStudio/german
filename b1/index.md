# B1

B1 is the first independent level. It starts from a secure A2 and builds the grammar for connected speech and writing: opinions, reasons, plans and past events in order.

## What learners know

- The passive, and the future with *werden*.
- The simple past and the past perfect, used in telling a story.
- Subordinate clauses of reason, result, purpose, time and concession.
- The Konjunktiv II for unreal conditions and polite requests.
- Infinitive constructions with *zu*, *lassen* and *brauchen*.

## What learners can do

- Give opinions and reasons, and explain plans briefly.
- Describe experiences, events and hopes in connected text.
- Tell the order of events in the past.
- Deal with most situations met in a German-speaking place.
- Follow the main points of clear standard speech on familiar topics.
