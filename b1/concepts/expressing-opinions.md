# Give an opinion

Stating a view and reacting to someone else's is the core move of any discussion, and German marks it with a small set of opening formulas.

## When to use it

- Discussions at work/school.
- Everyday decisions and preferences.

## Key phrases

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Ich finde, dass ... \| I think that ...` | neutral/common |
| {stack}`Meiner Meinung nach ... \| In my opinion ...` | slightly formal |
| {stack}`Ich bin der Meinung, dass ... \| I am of the opinion that ...` | formal |
| {stack}`Ich glaube/denke, ... \| I think ...` | informal/common |

---

## Examples

:::{div} ol-examples
**Ich finde, dass** Online-Unterricht praktisch ist.
: I think online classes are practical.

**Meiner Meinung nach** braucht man mehr Pausen.
: In my opinion, you need more breaks.

**Ich bin der Meinung, dass** Teamarbeit wichtig ist.
: I am of the opinion that teamwork is important.

**Ich denke**, wir sollten früher anfangen.
: I think we should start earlier.
:::

## Register and tone

Be polite: soften with vielleicht, etwas, eher; invite others’ views: Wie siehst du das?

## Mini practice

- Rewrite with a softer tone: „Ich habe recht.“ → „Ich glaube, ...“
- Choose a formal option for a short email.
