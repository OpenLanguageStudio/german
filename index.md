# Introduction

Open Language - German is a free and open coursebook for adults learning German, written in English. It explains how the language works one topic at a time, shows each point in short example sentences, and ends every article with a set of sentences to listen to. Anyone can read it, report a problem or suggest a change.

## Levels

The coursebook follows the levels of the Common European Framework of Reference, from A1 to B2. A1 covers introducing yourself, everyday needs and the basic sentence. A2 adds the past, comparisons and the cases in depth. B1 covers subordinate clauses, the passive and the Konjunktiv II for expressing opinions and reasons. B2 covers formal and precise German: nominal style, indirect speech and the less common connectors.

## Categories

Each level is divided into the same four categories. Concepts cover what to say in a situation, grammar covers how the language is built, writing gives model texts for everyday tasks, and vocabulary collects words on one theme. A level holds only the categories it has articles for.
