# Contracted prepositions

{term}`Contractions <contraction>` join a German preposition and an article into one word: *in dem* becomes *im*, and *zu der* becomes *zur*.

Eight contractions are common at A1. Six of them join a preposition to *dem* or *der* in the {term}`dative <dative case>`, the case these prepositions take. They say where someone is or where someone is heading: *im Kino*, *zum Arzt*. The other two, *ins* and *ans*, join a preposition to {term}`accusative <accusative case>` *das* and always show direction. Everyday German uses the short form by default.

## The contractions

| Contraction | Stands for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| im | in dem | im Kino, im Sommer |
| am | an dem | am Bahnhof, am Montag |
| zum | zu dem | zum Arzt |
| zur | zu der | zur Schule |
| vom | von dem | vom Markt |
| beim | bei dem | beim Arzt |
| ins | in das | ins Kino |
| ans | an das | ans Meer |

Six contract with dative *dem/der*; the last two contract with accusative *das* and carry direction.

## Being somewhere: *im*, *am*, *beim*

The dative contractions describe where someone is or when something happens.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich bin {prep}`im` Kino.
: I am at the cinema.

Sie ist {prep}`beim` Friseur.
: She is at the hairdresser's.

Ich bin {prep}`am` Bahnhof.
: I am at the station.
:::

## Heading somewhere: *zum*, *zur*, *ins*, *ans*

*Zum* and *zur* aim at people and buildings; *ins* and *ans* go INTO and TO places: *ins Kino, ans Meer*. Compare the pair: *Ich gehe **ins** Kino* (heading there) versus *Ich bin **im** Kino* (already there).

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich gehe {prep}`ins` Kino.
: I am going to the cinema.

Wir fahren {prep}`ans` Meer.
: We are going to the seaside.

Ich muss {prep}`zum` Bahnhof.
: I have to get to the station.
:::

:::{tip}
The full form (*in dem Kino*) is not wrong, but it sounds emphatic, as if pointing at one particular cinema. Everyday German contracts.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Ich gehe im Kino.* → Ich gehe **ins** Kino. (direction takes ins)
- *\*zur Bahnhof* → **zum** Bahnhof. (Bahnhof is {term}`masculine`)
- *\*Ich bin ins Büro.* → Ich bin **im** Büro. (being there takes im)
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- *im, am, zum, vom, beim* = preposition + dem; *zur* = zu + der.
- *ins, ans* = preposition + das, used for direction.
- The pair to feel: *ins Kino* (going) versus *im Kino* (being there).
- Contractions are the default in speech; full forms sound pointed.
:::
