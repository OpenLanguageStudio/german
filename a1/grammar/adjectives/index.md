# Adjectives

Adjectives describe things: big, new, cheap. At A1 they mostly follow *sein*, where they take no ending at all, and *gern* and *lieber* say what someone likes doing.

```{toctree}
:maxdepth: 1

praedikative-adjektive
gern-lieber
```
