# Say what you think

Giving an opinion in German runs mostly on one verb, *finden*. It gives the opinion, *Ich finde das gut*, asks for one, *Wie findest du …?*, and agrees, *Das finde ich auch*.

## When to use it

- Choosing photos for an article with a colleague
- Talking about a film with a friend on the way home
- Looking round a flat with a future flatmate
- Planning a trip and deciding how to travel

## Key phrases

### Giving an opinion

*Ich finde das gut* means "I think that's good", literally "I find that good". Any adjective can follow: *super*, *toll*, *schön*, *interessant*, *langweilig*, *nicht so gut*. *Doof* (silly, rubbish) is common between friends. Speech often starts with *das*: *Das finde ich schön*.

For a thing already named, a [pronoun in the accusative](/a1/grammar/pronouns/personalpronomen-akkusativ.md) takes its place. It is *ihn* for a *der* word, *sie* for a *die* word and *es* for a *das* word.

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Ich finde das gut. \| I think that's good.` | the basic opinion |
| {stack}`Das finde ich schön. \| I think that's nice.` | the same, with *das* first |
| {stack}`Ich finde ihn interessant. \| I find it interesting.` | *ihn* for a *der* word: *der Artikel* |
| {stack}`Ich finde es ein bisschen dunkel. \| I think it's a bit dark.` | *ein bisschen* softens it |
| {stack}`Ich glaube, ja. \| I think so.` | a guess about a fact, not a taste |

### Asking for an opinion

*Wie findest du …?* (What do you think of …?) asks about one thing. *Was meinst du?* and *Was denkst du?* (What do you think?) ask about a question or a plan. With *Sie* the verb changes: *Wie finden Sie das?*

:::{ol-examples}
:presenters: emilia
Wie findest du das Foto?
: What do you think of the photo?

Wie findest du meinen Artikel?
: What do you think of my article?

Was meinst du, regnet es morgen?
: What do you think, will it rain tomorrow?
:::

### Agreeing and disagreeing

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Das finde ich auch. \| I think so too.` | agrees with an opinion |
| {stack}`Stimmt. \| True.` | agrees with a fact |
| {stack}`Genau. \| Exactly.` | agrees, very common in speech |
| {stack}`Ich nicht. \| I don't.` | the short answer to *Ich finde …* |
| {stack}`Finde ich nicht. \| I don't think so.` | disagrees and stays friendly |
| {stack}`Ich weiß nicht. \| I don't know.` | no opinion yet |

## Opinions between friends

Between friends and colleagues of a similar age, opinions come fast and short. *Ich finde …* states one, and *Ich glaube, …* (I think, I believe) makes a guess.

:::{culture-note}
In German, a plain *Das finde ich nicht so gut* is often taken as honest rather than rude. Many people prefer a clear opinion to a polite hint.
:::

:title: Wie findest du das?

Wie findest du das Foto?
: What do you think of the photo?
Ich finde es gut.
: I think it's good.

Ich finde es ein bisschen dunkel.
: I think it's a bit dark.
Stimmt, das Licht ist nicht so gut.
: True, the light isn't very good.

Und das Foto hier?
: And this photo?
Das finde ich schön.
: I think that one's nice.

Ja, das finde ich auch.
: Yes, I think so too.
Gut, dann nehmen wir das.
: Good, then let's take that one.

Wie findest du meinen Artikel?
: What do you think of my article?
Ich finde ihn sehr interessant.
: I find it very interesting.

Ist er nicht zu lang?
: Isn't it too long?
Nein, finde ich nicht.
: No, I don't think so.

Wie findest du Berlin im Winter?
: What do you think of Berlin in winter?
Ich finde es kalt und grau.
: I think it's cold and grey.

Ich finde Nachtzüge toll.
: I think night trains are great.
Ich auch. Das Licht am Morgen ist so schön.
: Me too. The morning light is so beautiful.

Was denkst du, nehmen wir den Nachtzug?
: What do you think, shall we take the night train?
Ja, gute Idee!
: Yes, good idea!

Was meinst du, regnet es morgen?
: What do you think, will it rain tomorrow?
Ich glaube, ja.
: I think so.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- *Ich finde das gut* gives an opinion, and *Wie findest du …?* asks for one.
- A named thing becomes *ihn*, *sie* or *es*: *Ich finde ihn interessant.*
- *Das finde ich auch*, *Stimmt* and *Genau* agree. *Ich nicht* and *Finde ich nicht* disagree.
- *Ich glaube, …* makes a guess about a fact.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Wie magst du das Foto?* → **Wie findest du** das Foto? (English *How do you like it?* is *finden* in German)
- *\*Ich finde er super.* → Ich finde **ihn** super. (learn more in [Personal pronouns: accusative](/a1/grammar/pronouns/personalpronomen-akkusativ.md))
:::
