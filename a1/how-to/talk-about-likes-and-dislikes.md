# Talk about likes and dislikes

Likes and dislikes in German come in two shapes. An activity takes *gern*, as in *Ich spiele gern Fußball*, and a thing takes *mögen*, as in *Ich mag Kaffee*.

English uses *like* for both, so the step to learn is the choice: gern or mögen. A sentence with an activity verb, such as *spielen*, *kochen* or *reisen*, takes *gern*. A sentence about a thing, a food or a season takes *mögen*, and its negative needs *kein* or *nicht*.

## When to use it

- Chatting about hobbies with a new teammate after training
- Choosing what to eat with friends at a street-food market
- Answering a new flatmate who asks *Was machst du gern?*
- Telling a host which food to leave out

## Key phrases

### Activities with *gern*

*Gern* comes straight after the verb: *Ich koche gern*, literally "I cook gladly". *Sehr gern* makes it stronger. *Nicht gern* is the negative, and *nicht so gern* is the softer one people use most. [Gern, lieber, am liebsten](/a1/grammar/adjectives/gern-lieber.md) goes on to *lieber* (rather).

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Ich spiele gern Fußball. \| I like playing football.` | *gern* after the verb |
| {stack}`Ich spiele sehr gern Videospiele. \| I really like playing video games.` | *sehr gern*: a lot |
| {stack}`Ich lerne nicht so gern. \| I'm not so keen on studying.` | softer than *nicht gern* |
| {stack}`Was machst du gern? \| What do you like doing?` | the hobby question |

### Things with *mögen*

*Mögen* takes a thing: *ich mag*, *du magst*, *er* or *sie mag*, *Sie mögen*. With no article, the negative uses *kein*, as in *Ich mag keinen Fisch*. With *der*, *die* or *das*, *nicht* goes at the end: *Ich mag den Winter nicht*. [Negation](/a1/grammar/sentence-structure/negation.md) covers both.

:::{ol-examples}
:presenters: deniz
Nein, ich mag keinen Fisch.
: No, I don't like fish.

Nein, ich mag den Winter nicht.
: No, I don't like winter.

Tee mag ich nicht so.
: I'm not so keen on tea.
:::

For something seen or heard, such as a picture or a song, people also say *Das gefällt mir* (I like that). [Verbs with the dative](/a1/grammar/verbs/verben-mit-dativ.md) explains the *mir*.

## Hobbies and free time

A list of hobbies is a row of verbs, each followed by *gern*. For someone else the verb changes its ending and *gern* stays: *Meine Schwester reist gern*.

:::{audio-examples}
:track: a1/how-to/talk-about-likes-and-dislikes
:presenters: lukas
:title: Was ich gern mache
:title-en: What I like doing
:tags: likes and dislikes, leisure

Ich spiele gern Fußball.
: I like playing football.

Abends mache ich gern Musik.
: In the evening I like making music.

Ich höre gern elektronische Musik.
: I like listening to electronic music.

Ich spiele sehr gern Videospiele.
: I really like playing video games.

Ich koche gern, aber nur Pasta.
: I like cooking, but only pasta.

Ich lerne nicht so gern.
: I'm not so keen on studying.

Ich wasche nicht gern Wäsche.
: I don't like doing laundry.

Meine Schwester reist gern mit dem Zug.
: My sister likes travelling by train.

Sie fliegt nicht gern.
: She doesn't like flying.

Meine Mutter backt gern Kuchen.
: My mother likes baking cakes.

Mein Freund Deniz fährt gern Skateboard.
: My friend Deniz likes skateboarding.

Er zeichnet auch gern.
: He likes drawing too.

Mein Kollege Sam spielt auch gern Fußball.
: My colleague Sam likes playing football too.

Was machst du gern?
: What do you like doing?
:::

## Food, drink and seasons

*Magst du …?* (Do you like …?) asks about a thing. A short *Ja, sehr!* or *Nein, nicht so* is a full answer.

:::{audio-examples}
:track: a1/how-to/talk-about-likes-and-dislikes-things
:mode: pairs
:presenters: max, deniz
:title: Was magst du?
:title-en: What do you like?
:tags: likes and dislikes, food and drink, informal

Magst du Kaffee?
: Do you like coffee?
Ja, sehr! Ich bin Barista.
: Yes, a lot! I'm a barista.

Und Tee?
: And tea?
Tee mag ich nicht so.
: I'm not so keen on tea.

Magst du Döner?
: Do you like doner kebab?
Klar! Ich mag Streetfood.
: Sure! I like street food.

Magst du Fisch?
: Do you like fish?
Nein, ich mag keinen Fisch.
: No, I don't like fish.

Magst du den Winter?
: Do you like winter?
Nein, ich mag den Winter nicht.
: No, I don't like winter.

Und den Sommer?
: And summer?
Den Sommer mag ich sehr.
: I really like summer.

Was magst du nicht?
: What don't you like?
Regen! Ich mag keinen Regen.
: Rain! I don't like rain.

Gefällt dir das Bild?
: Do you like the picture?
Ja, das gefällt mir!
: Yes, I like it!
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- An activity takes *gern* after the verb: *Ich spiele gern Fußball.*
- A thing takes *mögen*: *Ich mag Kaffee.*
- *Nicht gern* and the softer *nicht so gern* say no to an activity. *Kein* or *nicht* says no to a thing.
- *Was machst du gern?* and *Magst du …?* are the questions.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Ich gern koche.* → Ich koche **gern**. (the verb comes second, *gern* after it; learn more in [Gern, lieber, am liebsten](/a1/grammar/adjectives/gern-lieber.md))
- *\*Ich mag nicht Fisch.* → Ich mag **keinen** Fisch. (learn more in [The negative article](/a1/grammar/articles/negativartikel.md))
:::
