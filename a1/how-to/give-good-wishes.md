# Give good wishes

Good wishes in German are short set phrases, one for each occasion. *Schönes Wochenende!* ends a working week, and *Gute Besserung!* goes to someone who is ill.

A wish usually comes back. The other person thanks and returns it with *Danke, gleichfalls!* or *Danke, dir auch!* Only a wish that belongs to one person, such as a birthday wish or *Gute Besserung*, gets a plain *Danke* or *Vielen Dank* in return.

## When to use it

- Saying goodbye to colleagues on a Friday afternoon
- Writing in a colleague's birthday card
- Sending a message to a friend who is ill
- Seeing off a friend before a long train journey
- Seeing in the New Year with friends

## Key phrases

### Everyday wishes

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Schönes Wochenende! \| Have a nice weekend!` | the Friday goodbye |
| {stack}`Schönen Abend noch! \| Have a nice evening!` | also *Schönen Tag noch!* |
| {stack}`Viel Spaß! \| Have fun!` | before a party, a film or a trip |
| {stack}`Viel Glück! \| Good luck!` | *Viel Erfolg!* is the usual wish for work or an exam |
| {stack}`Gute Besserung! \| Get well soon!` | to someone who is ill |
| {stack}`Gute Reise! \| Have a good trip!` | before a journey |
| {stack}`Guten Appetit! \| Enjoy your meal!` | before eating, to everyone at the table |

### Birthdays and holidays

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Alles Gute zum Geburtstag! \| Happy birthday!` | the most common birthday wish |
| {stack}`Herzlichen Glückwunsch! \| Congratulations!` | for a birthday, an exam, a new job |
| {stack}`Frohe Weihnachten! \| Merry Christmas!` | *Frohe Ostern!* at Easter |
| {stack}`Guten Rutsch! \| Happy New Year!` | only in the days before New Year |
| {stack}`Frohes neues Jahr! \| Happy New Year!` | from midnight on, and into January |

### Replies

| Phrase | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`Danke, gleichfalls! \| Thanks, you too!` | works with *du* and *Sie* |
| {stack}`Danke, dir auch! \| Thanks, you too!` | {bdg-light}`informal`; *Danke, Ihnen auch!* with *Sie* |
| {stack}`Vielen Dank! \| Thank you very much!` | for a wish only one person gets |

## Everyday wishes and the reply

Most everyday wishes are a noun with *schön*, *gut* or *viel* in front: *Schönes Wochenende!*, *Gute Reise!*, *Viel Spaß!* *Viel Spaß beim …* names the activity: *Viel Spaß beim Kochen!*

The reply thanks and sends the wish back. *Danke, gleichfalls!* works with anyone. *Danke, dir auch!* is for *du*, and *Danke, Ihnen auch!* for *Sie*. [Choose between du and Sie](choose-between-du-and-sie.md) covers the choice.

:presenters: tobias
Danke, gleichfalls!
: Thanks, you too!

Danke, dir auch!
: Thanks, you too!

Danke! Hoffentlich beißen die Fische.
: Thanks! I hope the fish bite.
:::

## Birthdays and holidays

Happy birthday in German is *Alles Gute zum Geburtstag!* or *Herzlichen Glückwunsch!* The birthday person says *Danke!* or *Vielen Dank!* [A birthday greeting](/a1/writing/postcard-2.md) shows the same wishes in a card.

*Guten Rutsch!* (a good slide into the New Year) is said in the days before New Year. From midnight on it is *Frohes neues Jahr!*

:::{culture-note}
Many Germans believe it brings bad luck to wish someone a happy birthday before the day. Cards, wishes and presents wait for the birthday itself. At a party the evening before, guests often wait until midnight.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- Each occasion has its set phrase: *Schönes Wochenende!*, *Gute Besserung!*, *Alles Gute zum Geburtstag!*
- *Danke, gleichfalls!* or *Danke, dir auch!* returns a wish that fits both people.
- A birthday wish or *Gute Besserung* gets a plain *Danke*.
- *Guten Rutsch!* comes before New Year and *Frohes neues Jahr!* after it.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Glücklichen Geburtstag!* → **Alles Gute** zum Geburtstag! (English *happy birthday* doesn't translate word for word)
- *\*Gutes Glück!* → **Viel** Glück! (luck comes in quantity: *viel*)
- *Guten Rutsch!* on 2 January → **Frohes neues Jahr!** (*Guten Rutsch* is only for the days before)
:::
