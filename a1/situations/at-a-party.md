# At a party

Sam has come to Julia's birthday party with her brother Lukas, who works with him. At a party where he knows almost nobody, he starts talking to Roman, an old friend of Julia's.

:::{how-to-links}
:::

## Hear it in practice

:::{audio-examples}
:track: a1/situations/at-a-party
:title: Auf einer Party
:title-en: At a Party
:mode: dialogue
:presenters: sam, roman
:tags: introductions, work

roman: Hallo! Ich bin Roman.
: Hi! I'm Roman.
sam: Hallo, Roman. Ich heiße Sam.
: Hi, Roman. My name is Sam.
roman: Freut mich, Sam.
: Nice to meet you, Sam.

roman: Woher kennst du Julia?
: How do you know Julia?
sam: Lukas ist mein Kollege. Er ist Julias Bruder.
: Lukas is my colleague. He's Julia's brother.
roman: Ach so! Julia und ich sind alte Freunde.
: Oh, I see! Julia and I are old friends.

roman: Und woher kommst du?
: And where are you from?
sam: Aus Australien, aus Melbourne.
: From Australia, from Melbourne.
roman: Melbourne! Wohnst du schon lange in Berlin?
: Melbourne! Have you lived in Berlin long?
sam: Ja, schon sechs Jahre. Und du? Woher kommst du?
: Yes, six years now. And you? Where are you from?
roman: Aus der Ukraine, aus Kiew. Aber ich wohne auch schon lange hier.
: From Ukraine, from Kyiv. But I've lived here a long time too.

sam: Und was machst du beruflich?
: And what do you do for a living?
roman: Ich bin Fotograf. Und du?
: I'm a photographer. And you?
sam: Ich bin Softwareentwickler.
: I'm a software developer.

roman: Möchtest du noch ein Bier?
: Would you like another beer?
sam: Ja, gern!
: Yes, please!
:::

## What to listen for

- Both use *du* from the first line. At a private party among people of a similar age, *Sie* would sound stiff.
- *Woher kennst du Julia?* is the classic party question: everyone there knows the host somehow.
- *Und du?* comes back three times. It returns a question in two words and keeps the conversation going.
- *Ach so!* shows that Roman has understood. It is the usual reply to new information.
