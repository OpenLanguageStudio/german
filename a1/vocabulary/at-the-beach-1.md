# At the beach

Beach vocabulary in German covers a day at the sea: sun, sand, water, ice cream, and the {term}`verbs <verb>` *schwimmen* and *liegen*.

Germans plan their beach holidays with care, and these words follow them from the packing list to the postcard. *Der Urlaub* is the word that most summer sentences turn on. The {term}`nouns <noun>` cover the scene and the kit, and the two verbs cover what people do there. Each noun comes with its article, because {term}`gender` is part of the word.

## The words

| Word | Notes |
|---|---|
| {stack}`der Strand \| beach` | plural *die Strände* |
| {stack}`das Meer \| sea` | plural *die Meere* |
| {stack}`die Sonne \| sun` | |
| {stack}`der Sand \| sand` | |
| {stack}`das Wasser \| water` | |
| {stack}`der Urlaub \| holiday` | plural *die Urlaube* |
| {stack}`das Eis \| ice cream, ice` | |
| {stack}`die Sonnenbrille \| sunglasses` | plural *die Sonnenbrillen* |
| {stack}`der Hut \| hat` | plural *die Hüte* |
| {stack}`das Handtuch \| towel` | plural *die Handtücher* |
| {stack}`schwimmen \| to swim` | |
| {stack}`liegen \| to lie (somewhere)` | |
| {stack}`warm \| warm` | |

Note the German quirk: *die Sonnenbrille* is {term}`singular`, one item, unlike English "sunglasses".

## Hear it in practice

One beach day from arrival to ice cream, with the holiday postcard vocabulary along for the ride.

:::{audio-examples}
:track: a1/vocabulary/at-the-beach-1
:title: Am Strand 1
:title-en: At the Beach 1

Der Strand ist heute voll.
: The beach is crowded today.

Das Meer ist blau.
: The sea is blue.

Die Sonne scheint.
: The sun is shining.

Wir sitzen in der Sonne.
: We are sitting in the sun.

Der Sand ist heiß.
: The sand is hot.

Das Wasser ist kalt.
: The water is cold.

Wir haben im August Urlaub.
: We are on holiday in August.

Der Urlaub war zu kurz.
: The holiday was too short.

Ich esse ein Eis am Strand.
: I am eating an ice cream on the beach.

Wo ist meine Sonnenbrille?
: Where are my sunglasses?

Die Sonnenbrille liegt in der Tasche.
: The sunglasses are in the bag.

Der Hut ist neu.
: The hat is new.

Ich schwimme gern im Meer.
: I like swimming in the sea.

Die Kinder schwimmen im Wasser.
: The children are swimming in the water.

Wir liegen im Sand.
: We are lying in the sand.

Das Handtuch liegt am Strand.
: The towel is lying on the beach.

Das Wasser ist heute warm.
: The water is warm today.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- The scene: *Strand, Meer, Sonne, Sand, Wasser*.
- The kit: *Sonnenbrille* (singular!), *Hut, Eis*.
- The verbs: *schwimmen* in the water, *liegen* on the sand.
- *Der Urlaub* is the word every German sentence about summer orbits.
:::
